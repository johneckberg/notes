# How to Create Executive Team Norms

## why?
Having a set of consistent norms helps an executive team with several things, including:

- Clarity on intentions 

- Increased trust 

- Time savings 

- Decreased politics 

- Setting a clear operational direction for the rest of the organization

- common goals are important to make work flow well. 

- break norms down into behaviors to implement them. Abstract norms into measurable behaviors

- commit to 5 norms or fewer

- Mutual accountability is essential for the plan to actually be implemented. For example, One team restricted device use in meetings and created a "norm bucket" where anyone distracted by their phone had to put in $5. 

I think a big part of this is predictability. What is someones role? If you consistently know what others are doing it helps you do your job better. Having static roles can also help people feel more valued in the workplace.
